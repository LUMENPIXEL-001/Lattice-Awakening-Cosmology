# LVC Program Statement

*LUMENPIXEL, May 2026*

---

</div>

LVC (Lagrangian Variable Cosmology) is a framework, not a model. Its mission is the data-accurate reconstruction of a localised modulation in the late-time expansion history of the universe, subject to four organising requirements: localisation, compactness, asymptotic recovery, and low-parameter structure.

Within this framework, several Lagrangian models coexist as members of the same family: the sine-Gordon kink reconstruction (v1.1), the Coleman-Weinberg R6 reconstruction (v1.2), the Lorentzian polynomial reconstruction (separate note), and the multi-rung phenomenological extension with non-minimal coupling (v1.3). Each is a realisation of the framework rather than its definition. No single realisation is privileged at the level of the framework itself; the choice among them is determined by data adequacy, structural elegance, and methodological preference.

The line of work archived under this account selects the scope-restricted path: the Lagrangian is preserved in its current elegant form (v1.1 sine-Gordon kink with v1.3 non-minimal coupling), and the framework's scope is explicitly limited to the late-modulation regime z ∈ [approximately 0.3, approximately 2]. High-redshift regimes such as the JWST early-galaxy regime are recorded as outside the current scope, as a direct consequence of the localisation requirement. Future extensions toward a unified cosmology by suitable Lagrangian modification remain possible and are left open as legitimate LVC realisations for future development.

The author is an independent researcher. The mission is not to secure timely academic acceptance but to record the structure as it stands, with full transparency about primitives, scope, and direction. The framework's value is measured by what it leaves on record for future verification against future data, not by present recognition. Reproduction scripts and data references are publicly archived; the results are independently verifiable by any reader at any time.

LVC awaits future cosmological data — DR3 BAO, future strong-lensing precision, and the maturing astrophysical interpretation of JWST high-redshift observations — to determine which of its members survives and which directions of extension prove productive. The author's role is to maintain the framework in a state ready for that future verification, not to anticipate its outcome.
